About

I'm a research engineer at DFKI in Saarbrücken, where I work on the SOOFI project as part of the post-training team. My focus is interpretability of new LLM architectures.

Before that I was an academic visitor at Oxford, working on learning stability and equilibria in multi-agent systems. At CSIC-IIIA in Barcelona I evaluated the moral capabilities of classifiers and LLMs, and built the LLM serving stack for a socially assistive robot.

I started out as a software engineer at Axión in Seville, working full-time while studying Mathematics and Computer Engineering.
